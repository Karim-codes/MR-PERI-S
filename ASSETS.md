- [Cabinet Grotesk source](https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/cabinet-grotesk)

The original full license is retained as `public/fonts/FONT-LICENSE.txt`, extracted from Fontshare's official Cabinet Grotesk download package. The font names and files have not been altered. This license covers the self-hosted ITF fonts. No paid font subscription is needed.
